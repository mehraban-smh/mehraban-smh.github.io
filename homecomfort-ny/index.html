What taking part involves

Three small things, about a minute a day

No diary to keep. You check in on your phone whenever you are at home, while one or two small sensors we lend you record the temperature and humidity.

  1. 1
    2 floors 3 people
    Once · about 5 min

    Tell us about your home

    Answer a few quick questions: the type of home, its size and floors, who lives with you, and how you heat and cool it. Mostly tapping, hardly any typing.

  2. 2
    A few times a day

    One tap: how do you feel?

    While you are home, a gentle reminder asks how you feel, from cold to hot, then a few quick taps about what you are wearing and doing. When nothing has changed, one tap confirms it all. You pick the hours, so nights stay quiet.

  3. 3
    Every week

    Watch your comfort story

    Your week fills with color as you check in. Keep a streak going, and see a personal summary of when you feel most comfortable at home.

The question you will answer

How do you feel right now?

  • Cold −3
  • Cool −2
  • Slightly cool −1
  • Neutral 0
  • Slightly warm +1
  • Warm +2
  • Hot +3

There are no right answers. Neutral, in the middle, means neither warm nor cool, and every answer is useful.

Who can take part

Most New Yorkers with a phone

You don't need a new home, a smart thermostat or any technical know-how. Owners and renters are both welcome.

Any kind of home counts
  • House
  • Townhouse
  • Apartment
  • Duplex
  • Mobile home
  • Dorm
You can take part if
  • You are 18 or olderAdults only.

  • You have a smartphoneAn iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later, or an Android phone with Chrome.

Your data

Private by design

You decide what to share, and you can stop at any time.

Your study code NY027

Your check-ins are filed under a code like this, never under your name. Your name and email are kept in a separate list, used to sign you in and to contact you about the study.

We collect
  • Your name and email, so we can sign you in and keep in touch
  • A short, mostly optional profile of you and your home, such as the kind of home you live in and how you heat and cool it
  • Your check-in answers, such as how warm or cool you feel, what you are wearing and which room you are in
  • Temperature and humidity from the small room sensors
  • Basic technical details about your phone, used to send you reminders
We never collect
  • Your phone’s GPS location
  • Anything from your contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • Anything from your other apps

  • Stored securely in the US

    Answers travel over an encrypted connection to a protected research database hosted in the United States.

  • No location tracking

    The app never uses GPS. Each check-in simply asks whether you are at home. Your ZIP code is optional and only matches check-ins to the local weather.

  • Pause or withdraw any time

    Pause reminders from the app whenever you like, or email us to leave the study.

  • Seen by the study team only

    Only the study team can see your answers, together with your name and email, to run the study and contact you. Anything published shows group results, never names.

  • Used only for research

    Your answers are used only for this study and the research that comes from it. They are never sold or used for advertising.

  • You stay in control

    Apart from your name, email and type of home, every question when you join can be skipped, and you choose the hours when reminders may arrive.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Anything else? Write to the study team.

How much time does it take?

Joining takes about five minutes, once: a few quick questions about you and your home. After that, most check-ins take about ten seconds, a few times a day while you are at home.

The study runs for two months.

What happens after I join?

You get your study code right away. The study team then confirms your registration, usually within a day, and the app lets you know when you can start checking in.

Why take part?

Your check-ins become part of a SUNY ESF research study on comfort in real homes, and help shape heating and cooling that suits the people who live there.

Along the way, the app shows you your own comfort story: how warm or cool you feel at different times of day.

Will there be any sensors in my home?

Yes, one or two small sensors. We will hand them to you to place in your home, where they record the temperature and humidity of the room. When the study ends, we will ask you to return them.

For check-ins, your phone is all you need.

How do reminders reach my phone?

Reminders are notifications from the Home Comfort NY check-in app. It runs in your phone’s browser, so there is nothing to download from an app store. Reminders only come during the home hours you choose, at most about once an hour.

To get them, add the check-in app to your home screen once. This information page is not the app, so open the check-in app first and add that page:

iPhone

  1. Open the check-in app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Home Comfort from your Home Screen, join or sign in there, and allow notifications.

Android

  1. Open the check-in app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Open it and allow notifications.

On iPhone, add the app before you join. The Home Screen app keeps its own copy of your answers, separate from Safari, and reminders only work in the Home Screen app. Already joined in Safari? Open the Home Screen app, choose I have registered before and enter the same email. Your study code comes back, and the check-ins you already sent stay saved with the study; only the history shown in Safari stays behind in Safari.

On iPhone, reminders need iOS 16.4 or later.

What if I'm away from home?

Only check in while you are at home. If a reminder arrives while you are out, tap No, I'm out and reminders pause for a few hours, or until you are back.

Going away for a few days? Turn reminders off in the app, and back on when you return.

Who sees my answers?

Only the study team. Your check-ins are stored under your study code, and your name and email sit in a separate list that only the team can open. When the team signs in to the study’s research dashboard, they see the two together, so they can follow the study and contact you.

Anything we publish shows group results, never names or individual homes.

How do I withdraw?

Email info@mehraban.uk or smirzabeigi@esf.edu, and we will arrange with you how to return the sensors. If you only need a break, pause or turn off reminders in the app instead.
