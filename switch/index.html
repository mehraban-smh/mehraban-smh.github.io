Swansea University · EPSRC SWITCH to Net Zero Buildings

AI-integrated building management for personalised thermal comfort

Developing an AI-integrated building management framework that predicts individual occupants’ thermal comfort and translates those predictions into personalised building control recommendations for net-zero buildings across Swansea Bay and South Wales.

The research combines real-world monitoring in occupied buildings, explainable AI, and transfer learning to develop comfort models that can learn from occupant and environmental data, explain the factors driving individual comfort responses, and adapt to new buildings and populations where labelled comfort data are limited.

Working with Energy Carbon and regional partners towards more occupant-responsive building operation that balances personal comfort, energy performance, and net-zero objectives.

How the study works

  1. A small sensor in your home. A Tapo T315 thermometer and hygrometer records the temperature and humidity of your main living space every few minutes. It has an e-ink display, needs no attention, and is collected at the end of the study.
  2. Short comfort check-ins on your phone. While you are at home, the check-in app asks every half hour how you feel: warm or cool, what you are wearing, what you have been doing. Most check-ins take under twenty seconds, and one tap confirms everything when nothing has changed.
  3. A comfort model that learns you. Your answers are matched with the sensor readings to learn the conditions in which you feel comfortable, so heating and ventilation can be steered around people rather than around a fixed setpoint.

Taking part

Open the comfort check-in

Use the personal link from your welcome message the first time, so the app knows your participant code. After that, add it to your home screen so it opens like an app:

iPhone
  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open it from the home screen and allow notifications when asked.
Android
  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Open it from the home screen and allow notifications when asked.

Your data

  • You are identified only by a participant code. Your name and address are never stored with your answers.
  • The app asks whether you are at home before each check-in. It does not track your location.
  • Check-ins are stored on a secure database in the UK and are used only for this research.
  • You can pause check-ins from the app at any time, and you can withdraw from the study without giving a reason by contacting the team.
