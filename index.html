About

Healthier buildings, taught by data

I am a PhD researcher at the School of Construction, Property and Surveying, London South Bank University, where my doctoral project develops AI-integrated digital twins for the early detection and prediction of damp and mould in UK social housing, in collaboration with Wates Group. The aim is practical: give housing providers a way to see moisture risk building up in a home weeks before it becomes visible on a wall.

Alongside the PhD, I work as a Research Associate at Swansea University on the EPSRC SWITCH programme, applying explainable AI and transfer learning to thermal comfort prediction in net-zero buildings, so that comfort models trained where data is rich can serve buildings and communities where data is scarce.

My research sits at the meeting point of construction engineering and machine learning: BIM and digital twins, building performance simulation, and the automation of workflows that the industry still does by hand.

Research

Current projects

PhD · LSBU × Wates Group

Digital twins for damp & mould prediction in social housing

Combining IoT sensing, building physics and machine learning into a digital twin that flags moisture and mould risk in occupied homes early, supporting healthier housing and smarter maintenance across the UK social housing stock.

Physics-informed, AI-integrated digital twin framework for real-time mould growth prediction — view full size.

Research Associate · Swansea · EPSRC SWITCH

Explainable AI for thermal comfort in net-zero buildings

Developing transfer learning and explainable AI methods that predict occupant thermal comfort across different buildings and climates from limited local data, informing the design and operation of net-zero homes.
